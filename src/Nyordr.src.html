Rates (per interval)

Base, EP, true-up, ramp and helpdesk are shown at starting prices; every dollar of escalation is in the Inflation column. EP counts are auto-projected from growth. Override any interval directly in the table (or via upload).

Inflation / escalator

Inflation runs on its own schedule. Ramp steps never trigger it. Example: $10k/yr, +$5k ramp every 6 months, 3% yearly from month 13:
• Prior period total: mo 1–6 $10k, mo 7–12 $15k, mo 13: $15k × 1.03 + $5k = $20,450
• Starting price only: mo 13: $10k × 1.03 + $10k = $20,300
• Current total: mo 13: ($15k + $5k) × 1.03 = $20,600
Rows with an inflation increase are marked ▲.

Billing & true-up

Between true-ups you're billed the commitment. Overage (actual − committed) × that interval's EP rate accrues and is billed at the true-up, plus premium. A final true-up always runs in the last interval.

Price ramp

Each step adds the amount on top of the prior steps and stays for the rest of the term. Month 1 is the contract start; a step that lands mid-interval is prorated. How ramp increases interact with inflation is set under Inflation.

Helpdesk services

Hours, visits and trip fees are per month or per year (as selected) and are scaled to the contract interval. Override remote/onsite hours for any interval in the table or via upload (remote_hours, onsite_hours columns).

Upload format

CSV or Excel. A Schedule table (interval, endpoints, other_cost, note) sets per-interval EPs and extra costs. A Settings table (setting, value) sets any field above. An Excel file can hold both as separate sheets.
